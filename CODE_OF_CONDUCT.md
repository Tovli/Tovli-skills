# Code of Conduct

Tovli Skills welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and points of view. Participate with respect and keep discussion focused on improving the skills and their documentation.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, and sharing another person's private information without consent.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. They may also restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact options available on the repository's hosting platform. For security concerns, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
